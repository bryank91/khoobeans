Application description

KhooLearns is a custom content-publishing app by Bryan at Khoobeans, focused on education, learning, artificial intelligence, and technology. It supports creating, organizing, and publishing educational content to help make new ideas and practical knowledge easier to explore.

  • Create and organize educational posts and learning resources.
  • Share insights, explanations, and practical examples about AI and technology.
  • Publish content through connected services with the account owner's authorization.

Google account authorization

KhooLearns uses Google authorization to connect a Google account to supported publishing features. Google shows the permissions being requested before you approve access. Authorization allows the app to perform the actions covered by those permissions on your behalf; it does not give KhooLearns your Google password.

This is the public information page, not a sign-in or publishing interface. You can review or revoke the app's access at any time in your Google Account connections.
