Privacy Policy

KhooLearns · Effective 1 November 2026

About this policy

This policy applies to KhooLearns, a custom application operated by Bryan at Khoobeans for creating and publishing educational content about learning, AI, and technology. Contact bryan@khooserver.com with privacy questions or requests.

Information the app handles

  • Account information: When you connect a Google account, the app may receive basic account information, such as your account identifier, name, and email address, where included in the permissions you approve.
  • Authorization credentials: OAuth access tokens and, where provided, refresh tokens allow the app to maintain the connection and perform authorized actions. The app does not receive your Google password.
  • Publishing content: The app handles the content and associated details you provide or select for publishing, along with information returned by the connected service for those actions. Access to Google service data is limited to the permissions you grant.
  • Support communications: If you contact us, we receive your email address and any information you include in your message.
  • Website requests: This public site is hosted on GitHub Pages. The hosting provider may process technical request information, such as IP addresses, for delivery, security, and operation of the site. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

How information is used

Information is used to connect your account, provide the publishing features you authorize, maintain the service's security and reliability, troubleshoot problems, and respond to support or deletion requests. Only approve permissions that you are comfortable granting; the Google consent screen identifies the access requested for your connection.

Sharing and disclosure

Content and the information necessary to publish it are sent to the connected service when you use publishing features. Published content may be public depending on the destination and your settings. Service providers may process information as necessary to operate the app, subject to appropriate confidentiality and security obligations. Information may also be disclosed when required by law or to address fraud, abuse, or security threats.

We do not sell personal information or use Google user data for advertising. Google user data is not used to train or improve generalized artificial intelligence or machine learning models. Educational content about AI does not change these restrictions.

Google API user data

KhooLearns' use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Google user data is used only to provide or improve the user-facing features you authorize. Human access to that data is limited to circumstances permitted by that policy, such as with your affirmative agreement to view specific data for support, for necessary security investigations, or to comply with applicable law.

Retention and security

Account information, authorization credentials, and publishing data are kept only as long as needed to provide the connected features or meet legal and security obligations. Credentials should be treated as confidential. We use reasonable safeguards to protect information, but no method of storage or transmission is completely secure.

Your choices and deletion

You can revoke Google access at any time through your Google Account connections. Revoking access prevents future authorized access through that connection; it does not automatically remove previously published content or all previously stored information.

To request deletion of information held by KhooLearns, including stored account data, credentials, and publishing data, email bryan@khooserver.com from the connected account where possible. We may need to verify ownership before acting. We will delete information that is no longer needed, except information required for legal or security obligations. Content already published to a third-party service must also be managed or deleted through that service.

Children's privacy

KhooLearns is not directed to children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided personal information, please contact us so we can address it.

Policy changes

Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date. Material changes affecting Google user data will be communicated and any additional consent obtained as required by applicable policies and law.
