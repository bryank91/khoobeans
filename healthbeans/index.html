What HealthBeans does

  • Reads sleep, activity, heart rate, and workout data from Apple Health.
  • Computes daily and hourly aggregates on-device.
  • Generates wellness insights by sending only those aggregates to an AI provider you choose.

Frequently asked questions

How do I get an API key?

HealthBeans uses your own OpenRouter or MiniMax API key — yours, not ours. Sign up at openrouter.ai or MiniMax.io, create a key, and paste it into the Configure tab in HealthBeans. Keys are stored only in your iOS Keychain.

Does HealthBeans share my raw health data?

No. Raw HealthKit samples never leave your iPhone. Only the daily and hourly aggregates you approve are sent to your selected AI provider.

How do I delete my local data?

Open HealthBeans → Settings → Delete local data. This clears your stored AI keys and preferences. Your data in Apple Health is not affected.

Is HealthBeans a medical device?

No. HealthBeans provides general wellness information only and does not diagnose or treat any condition. Always consult a qualified clinician for medical decisions.

Privacy summary

  • Raw HealthKit samples stay on this device.
  • Only on-device aggregates are sent to your chosen AI provider.
  • API keys are stored in the iOS Keychain, accessible only when the device is unlocked.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no third-party SDKs.
